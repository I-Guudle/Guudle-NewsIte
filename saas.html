Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What services does GUUDLE provide?

SaaS products, AI features, fintech software, enterprise systems, automation, websites, mobile apps, and the cloud infrastructure those products run on.

Can GUUDLE build custom SaaS applications?

Yes. Multi-tenant products, billing, and admin are scoped as a project. The GUUDLE workspace is a separate published product with its own plans.

Do you develop Android and iOS applications?

Yes. Native Android, native iOS, and cross-platform builds are available. Store approval still depends on the platform owner.

Can you integrate AI into existing systems?

Yes, through an API or a feature inside the product, with a review step where the workflow needs one. Results depend on the data and the task.

Do you provide fintech software development?

Yes, as software engineering. GUUDLE does not present itself here as a licensed bank, payment institution, or investment firm.

Can you integrate third-party APIs?

Yes, where the provider offers an API and your account is allowed to use it.

Do you offer cloud hosting?

Yes. Hosting is agreed as part of the project or as a separate infrastructure engagement.

Do you provide ongoing technical support?

Yes, when it is included in the plan or written into the support agreement. It is not assumed after every launch.

Can businesses request customised solutions?

Yes. Professional and Business packages on this page are quoted. Describe the workflow and we will reply with a scope.

How can customers request a quotation?

Use Contact on this site, or the Request a Quote and Book a Demo buttons. A quotation is a written scope, not a charge taken on this page.
