Enterprise Mobile Capabilities

How we design, secure, and deploy enterprise mobile platforms — from identity and offline field workflows to MDM rollout and executive analytics.

Identity

Enterprise SSO & Workforce Identity

Single sign-on and role-based access for every employee app.

We integrate Azure AD, Okta, and SAML identity providers so your workforce signs in once and accesses approved apps with MFA, conditional access, and session policies aligned to IT governance.

Azure ADOktaSAML 2.0MFA
  • Role-based permissions mapped to HR and directory groups
  • Biometric unlock with secure token refresh and session timeout
  • Conditional access for device posture and geo-fencing
02
Field Ops

Offline-First Field Operations

Productivity continues when connectivity drops on site.

Field teams capture work orders, inspections, and signatures without signal. Local-first storage syncs when back online with conflict resolution, audit timestamps, and supervisor approval queues.

SQLite / RealmBackground syncGPS capture
  • Queued uploads with retry and supervisor conflict review
  • Digital signatures, photo evidence, and barcode scanning offline
  • Route-aware sync prioritization for low-bandwidth regions
03
Device Mgmt

MDM & Device Governance

Deploy and enforce policies across corporate device fleets.

Apps are packaged for Microsoft Intune, Jamf, and VMware Workspace ONE with managed configurations, app wrapping, and compliance checks before sensitive data is exposed on device.

IntuneJamfApp configDLP
  • Managed app configuration pushed from your MDM console
  • Remote wipe and data isolation for BYOD and COPE models
  • Certificate pinning and jailbreak/root detection hooks

Security

Secure Data & Regulatory Compliance

Encryption, audit trails, and controls for regulated industries.

We architect for HIPAA, SOC 2, GDPR, and PCI where applicable — with encrypted storage, TLS 1.3, tamper-evident logs, and data residency options for healthcare, finance, and public sector clients.

HIPAASOC 2GDPRAt-rest encryption
  • End-to-end encryption for PHI, PII, and payment-adjacent flows
  • Immutable audit logs exportable to SIEM and compliance tooling
  • Data minimization and retention policies enforced in-app

Integration

ERP & Line-of-Business Connectors

Mobile workflows wired into systems teams already run.

We connect mobile front-ends to SAP, Salesforce, ServiceNow, Dynamics, and custom APIs via secure middleware — so approvals, inventory, and case updates stay synchronized with source-of-truth systems.

SAPSalesforceServiceNowREST / GraphQL
  • Event-driven sync with idempotent API handlers and retry queues
  • Webhook and polling strategies matched to vendor rate limits
  • Staging environments mirroring production integration contracts
