Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What is legacy modernisation?

It is the work of upgrading applications, databases and infrastructure that are difficult to change, secure or connect, while the organisation keeps operating.

Why should organisations modernise legacy systems?

Older systems become harder to maintain, integrate and protect. Modernisation reduces those limits so software can change with less risk.

Can existing applications be upgraded without rebuilding them?

Often yes. An API layer, refactoring, or moving one function at a time can be enough. A full rebuild is used when the current system cannot meet the requirement.

Do you provide cloud migration services?

Yes. GUUDLE plans which applications and infrastructure are suitable to move, then migrates them in stages.

Can legacy systems integrate with modern applications?

Yes, where the older system can share data or functions through an interface, a file exchange, or a new API layer.

How do you minimise business disruption?

Changes are introduced in stages, tested before cutover, and scheduled so critical operations can continue.

Do you provide ongoing technical support?

Yes, when support is written into the agreement. It is arranged as part of the engagement.
